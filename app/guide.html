1. Getting started

ଆରମ୍ଭ

Install the app

  • Android app (best): open the download link Kartikeswar.apk, tap Install and, if asked, allow "Install unknown apps" once. When the app asks, allow SMS and Notifications. These are needed for SMS reminders and alarms.
  • Any phone (browser): open the app link in Chrome, then ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Sign in

  1. Type the ID and password the committee sent you on WhatsApp (for example ID kuna).
  2. Tap Sign in.
  3. Members added with a Gmail address can open the app in a browser and tap Gmail member? Sign in with Google at the bottom.

Change your password

More → Members → Change my password: type your current password, then the new one twice (at least 6 characters).

Language

Tap More → English / ଓଡ଼ିଆ at the top to switch the app's language. This only changes your phone.

Good to know: the app works without internet. Entries are saved on your phone and go up by themselves when the network comes back.

2. The Home screen

ମୂଳ ପୃଷ୍ଠା
  • Collected / Spent / Balance for this year's puja (balance includes last year's leftover money).
  • Compared with last year: progress bar against last year's total.
  • Active now: which member is contacting which donor right now.
  • Today's calls: donors someone promised to call back today.
  • Follow-up: how many have not paid yet, and money still to come from promises.
  • Checklist jobs due in the next 3 days, and vendor dues.
  • Cash in hand: how much money each member is holding.

The green Share today's summary on WhatsApp button makes a ready message for the committee group: today's collections, total, cash in hand and pending promises.

3. Adding a collection

ଚାନ୍ଦା ଆଦାୟ ଲେଖିବା
  1. On the donor's card tap Paid, or tap the big + → Collection.
  2. Check the name (start typing and pick from the list), the amount, and choose Cash or UPI / Online.
  3. For UPI, choose UPI paid into: the main committee QR or a member's own UPI.
  4. Optional: Take photo or Gallery / screenshot to attach the payment screenshot.
  5. Tap Save collection.

Every collection gets a receipt number like KP-2026-014 and its own receipt page with a Download PDF button.

  • Android app: the receipt SMS with the link goes to the donor automatically from your SIM.
  • Tap Send thank-you on WhatsApp to also send it on WhatsApp.
Namaskar Kuna Rout 🙏 We have received your contribution of ₹1,500 for Kartikeswar Puja 2026. Thank you for your support! Total given ₹2,000 of ₹3,000 · Pending ₹1,000 Receipt No: KP-2026-014 Receipt: https://…/receipt.html?r=…
Always save the money the same day. Cash in hand and the daily summary are only right if every collection is entered.

4. Part payments

କିସ୍ତିରେ ଦେବା

Some donors promise an amount and pay it in parts, for example ₹3,000 as ₹500, then ₹1,500, then ₹1,000.

  1. When they promise: Update → Promised to give → Promised amount 3000 → Save.
  2. Each time they pay, tap Paid (it says Add payment after the first part). The amount still due is filled in already; change it to what they actually gave.
Kuna Rout
₹2,000 of ₹3,000₹1,000 due
  • The donor stays in the Part paid list until the full amount comes in, then moves to Paid.
  • Reminders tell them what is pending, and the pay link asks for just that amount.
  • Each receipt shows the running total. The last one says Fully paid ✓.
  • If they say they can't give the rest: Update → tick "Accept ₹2,000 as final". They move to Paid and stop getting reminders.

5. Following up with donors

ବାକି ଚାନ୍ଦା

The Follow-up tab lists every donor. Messages to donors in: ଓଡ଼ିଆ / English above the list picks the language of reminders and thank-you messages sent from your phone. Tap ✎ next to a donor's name to fix their name, phone or past amounts.

Use the chips at the top to filter:

ListWho is in it
To follow upEveryone who has not fully paid (except "Not giving")
Call todayCall-backs due today or earlier
PromisedSaid they will give, nothing paid yet
Part paidPaid some of what they promised
MineDonors assigned to you
Not contactedNobody has reached them yet
Paid / Not givingDone for this year

Buttons on each donor

  • Remind opens WhatsApp with a ready message, including last year's amount and a pay link.
  • Call dials the donor. When you come back to the app it asks "How did the call go?": tap Paid now, No answer (try again tomorrow), Promised, Call again later (pick date and time) or Not giving.
  • UPI QR shows the QR to scan in person, or sends the pay link.
  • Update sets status, promised amount, call-back date, who will follow up, and a note.
  • Paid adds a collection.

Reminder queue: many donors quickly

  1. Tap Start reminders, choose a list, and tap Start.
  2. Tap the green button → press Send in WhatsApp → come back. The next donor appears by itself.
  3. Use Skip for anyone you don't want to message.

Who is on a donor

When any member reminds, calls or updates a donor, everyone sees it on the card, e.g. "Kuna sent a reminder · 2:30 pm" (green dot = in the last hour). It never blocks you. It only helps avoid sending the same donor three messages in one day.

Messages already include gentle encouragement: how many families have given so far, the usual amount, and the donor title they can reach (Sevak, Pradhan Sevak, Maha Sevak). Nobody who hasn't paid is ever named.

6. SMS reminders (Android app only)

SMS ମନେ ପକାଇବା
  1. Follow-up → Send SMS reminders.
  2. Pick a list. The app shows how many SMS it will use and a preview.
  3. Tap Start and keep the app open. SMS go one by one from your own SIM, a few seconds apart.
Watch your SMS pack. An Odia SMS uses about 2 SMS from your pack. There is a daily limit (90 by default) so your number does not get blocked by the operator.

7. UPI, QR and pay links

UPI ଓ QR
  • In person: tap UPI QR on the donor and let them scan it.
  • Far away: the reminder already has a pay link. It opens a page with the QR and a Copy UPI ID button.
  • After they pay, ask for the screenshot and add the collection as UPI / Online, attaching the screenshot.

8. Your own UPI / QR (optional)

ନିଜ UPI

The committee has no bank account, so money goes to members' personal UPI. If donors should pay into your account:

  1. More → My UPI & QR.
  2. Type your UPI ID, upload your QR image (screenshot from GPay / PhonePe), and tap Save.

From then on your reminders, pay links and QR screen use your account. Others keep using the main QR. To go back, tap Stop using my UPI.

Remember: UPI money in your account counts in your cash in hand. Hand it over like cash (see below).

9. Expenses and vendors

ଖର୍ଚ୍ଚ ଓ ଦୋକାନୀ
  1. + → Expense → category (Murti, Tent, Flower…), item, amount, who paid.
  2. Attach a photo of the bill if you have one.

More → Vendors & budget is for big bookings paid in parts (Murti, Tent, DJ). Add the vendor with the agreed total and each advance payment. The app shows what is still due. You can also set a budget per category.

10. Cash in hand and handovers

କାହା ପାଖରେ କେତେ ଟଙ୍କା
  • A cash collection adds to the person who received it.
  • UPI money adds to whoever's account it went into ("Main UPI account" or the member).
  • An expense subtracts from whoever paid.

When you give money to another member: + → Cash handover → from, to, amount. This is also how last year's leftover balance is handed out.

11. Puja checklist

ପୂଜା କାମ ତାଲିକା

More → Puja checklist has the main jobs from last year (book priest, order Murti, tent, DJ, flowers, Bhoga…) with due dates counted back from the puja date. Assign a job to someone, and tick it when it's done. Add your own with + → Checklist task.

12. Alarms and home-screen widget (Android app)

ଆଲାର୍ମ ଓ ୱିଜେଟ୍
  • Call-back alarms: when you set "Call again on" with a time, your phone rings a reminder then, even if the app is closed. It rings only for donors assigned to you or to nobody.
  • Checklist alarms: at 9 am on the due date of your jobs.
  • Widget: long-press your home screen → Widgets → Kartikeswar. Shows today's collection, the total, and how many are yet to pay.

13. Fixing mistakes

ଭୁଲ ସୁଧାରିବା
  • Entries tab → tap the entry → change and Save changes. The receipt page updates too.
  • Wrong entry? Open it → Delete entry. A deleted collection stays in the list as Cancelled with its number (so numbers have no gaps), and its receipt page shows CANCELLED.
  • Nothing is ever truly erased, so a mistake can always be recovered.

14. Settings (for the committee)

ସେଟିଂସ୍

Change these only after the committee agrees. They affect everyone.

  • More → Year, UPI & WhatsApp messages: puja year and date, last year's balance, the main UPI ID and QR, whose account the main UPI is, message language and wording, SMS settings, gentle nudges and donor titles.
  • More → Members: add or remove committee members. Type a name and a short ID (like kuna) and the login is made at once. Leave the password empty for a random one. Add many members takes one person per line: Name, ID, password. Afterwards tap Send next to each person to WhatsApp them their ID, password and the app link. A Gmail address instead of an ID means they sign in with Google.
  • More → Donors & phone numbers: add donors or paste many numbers at once (Name, 9876543210). Tap a donor (or ✎ on Follow-up) to change the name, phone or past years' amounts. If the same person is in the list twice with different spellings, change one name to exactly the other and the app offers to merge them. Their past amounts are added together.
  • More → Download / Export: Excel (CSV) files of everything, for the year-end accounts.

15. Questions

ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ
I saved a collection without internet. Where is the receipt?

The receipt number is given automatically once your phone is online again, and the receipt SMS goes then. You can send it on WhatsApp later from the Entries tab.

Two of us reminded the same donor. Is that a problem?

No, but check the line on the donor card ("Kuna sent a reminder · 2:30 pm") before messaging, and use Mine / "Who will follow up" to split donors.

The donor paid more than they promised.

Just enter what they gave. They show as fully paid, and the receipt shows the total.

Can donors see our collection list?

No. Only committee members can open the data. A donor's receipt link shows only their own receipt.

A member forgot their password.

Ask whoever manages the Firebase console to delete that login (Firebase console → Authentication → Users), then add the member again in More → Members with a new password.

SMS didn't send.

Check that the app has SMS permission (Phone settings → Apps → Kartikeswar → Permissions), that you have SMS balance, and that you haven't reached the daily limit. You can always send on WhatsApp instead.

Do I need to install a new APK when the app changes?

Usually not. The app loads the latest version from the website by itself. A new APK is shared only when the Android part changes.
